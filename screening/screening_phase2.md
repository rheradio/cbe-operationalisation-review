## Criterion codes

| Code | Criterion |
|------|-----------|
| I1 | CBE/CBL paradigm structurally operative |

### Exclusion (ANY is sufficient)
| Code | Criterion |
|------|-----------|

| E2 | Workforce/professional-certification "competency" only — no student HE assessment |
| E3 | No assessment design (curriculum mapping, framework, accreditation, perception survey) |

### 004
**Title:** Feedforward Enhanced Control System to Pursue Mathematical Competencies Achievement in Engineering Education (2024)
**Decision:** INCLUDE | **Criteria:** I1–I5
**Rationale:** Presents the TAC (Topics × Activities × Competencies) Cuboid — a 3D matrix in which each cell aijk encodes the impact of activity Aik on competency Cj for topic Tk, with weights wik. Three TAC variants (personal pTAC, objective oTAC, master mTAC) form an explicit aggregation chain: binary per-competency evaluations are weighted into pTAC, compared against oTAC targets, and gaps trigger the FECS personalised recovery system. Competencies are grounded in the SEFI Mathematics Working Group framework. Applied to Mathematics I in the Aerospace Engineering degree at UPV Valencia with 47 volunteer students and empirical pre/post data. The named competencies, named activities, explicit weight formulas, and traceable path from task performance to competency verdict satisfy all five inclusion criteria.

### 006
**Title:** Assessing Work-Based Learning in the Senior Years of a Software Engineering Graduate Apprenticeship Program (2025)
**Decision:** EXCLUDE | **Criteria:** E10
**Rationale:** Describes a graduate apprenticeship portfolio in which apprentices claim UK-SPEC competency dimensions A–E with evidence, reviewed by academics alongside employer recommendation, with a 30% portfolio weight. Miller's pyramid is invoked conceptually but no scoring formula links the five dimensions to a pass/fail or grade outcome. E10 applies: criteria per competency level and traceable aggregation are absent.

### 015
**Title:** Competency Based Education and Continuous Assessment in Laboratory to acquire higher order learning levels (2022)
**Decision:** INCLUDE | **Criteria:** I1–I5
**Rationale:** Presents the OLAC model with five Assessment Components (AC1 Written Work, AC2 Fundamental Knowledge, AC3 Questions Answered, AC4 Interaction, AC5 Punctuality), each graded 0–5 per experiment across 10 experiments. The aggregation chain is fully specified: TAC_i = mean of SAC_i over experiments; CO_j = mean of contributing TAC_i values; PO_k = mean of contributing CO_j values, with explicit examples (CO1 = (TAC1+TAC3+TAC5)/3; PO1 = (CO1+CO3+CO4)/3) and a 50% mastery threshold. Mapping tables link each AC to the COs and POs it feeds. Applied across three engineering streams over five academic years with more than 8,000 data points. The complete AC→CO→PO hierarchy with arithmetic formulas satisfies all five inclusion criteria.
